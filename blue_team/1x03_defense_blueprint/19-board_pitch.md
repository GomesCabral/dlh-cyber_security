# 19. Board Pitch – MedDefense Health Systems

## Current State

Over the past four weeks, MedDefense completed its first comprehensive cybersecurity assessment. The results show that while critical clinical services remain operational, the organization faces significant cyber risk due to a flat network, limited monitoring, inconsistent patch management, and insufficient protection for critical systems. Without corrective action, the current security posture should be considered **High Risk**.

## The Risk

If MedDefense takes no action, a ransomware attack initiated through the VPN could encrypt the Electronic Health Record (EHR) system, disrupt patient care, expose sensitive medical records, and halt billing operations. Based on our quantitative risk analysis, this scenario represents an **Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE) of approximately $1.7 million**, far exceeding the proposed investment.

## The Plan

We recommend investing **$120,000** to implement the highest-priority security improvements identified during the assessment. The first phase focuses on **Network Segmentation**, **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)**, and **Centralized Security Monitoring (Wazuh SIEM)**, supported by immutable backups and an enterprise firewall for the Westside Clinic. These controls directly address the organization's highest risks, significantly reduce the likelihood of ransomware, limit attacker movement, improve incident detection, and strengthen recovery capabilities. Together, they are expected to reduce MedDefense's overall cyber risk by more than **75%**, while aligning the organization with the NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 and CIS Controls v8.

## The Return

For an investment of **$120,000**, MedDefense avoids approximately **$1.7 million in expected annual cyber losses**—a return of roughly **14 dollars avoided for every dollar invested**. This investment not only protects patient safety and regulatory compliance but also preserves operational continuity, financial stability, and the trust of patients, staff, and the Board.
