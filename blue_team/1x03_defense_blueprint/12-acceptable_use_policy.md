# 12. Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)
## MedDefense Health Systems

**Document ID:** AUP-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Deputy CISO (James Chen)  
**Approved By:** CEO  
**Effective Date:** Immediately upon approval  
**Review Frequency:** Annually or following major security incidents.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) is to protect MedDefense Health Systems' information systems, patient data, employees, and business operations by defining acceptable and prohibited use of organizational technology resources.

This policy supports MedDefense's cybersecurity program, aligns with NIST CSF 2.0, CIS Controls v8, and ISO/IEC 27001, and helps reduce the risks identified during the Security Posture Assessment, Threat Landscape Assessment, and Vulnerability Assessment.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees
- Physicians
- Nurses
- Contractors
- Volunteers
- Temporary staff
- Third-party service providers with network access

It applies to all MedDefense technology resources including:

- Desktop computers
- Laptops
- Clinical workstations
- Medical devices connected to the network
- Servers
- Mobile devices
- Email systems
- VPN
- Wireless networks
- Cloud services
- Internet access
- Storage devices

---

# 3. Acceptable Use of Systems

Employees are permitted to use MedDefense systems only for legitimate business and healthcare purposes.

Authorized users may:

- Access only systems necessary for their job duties.
- Use approved clinical applications.
- Access patient records only when required for patient care.
- Use MedDefense email for business communications.
- Connect remotely only through the approved VPN.
- Store business information only in approved corporate systems.
- Report suspected security incidents immediately.

Users are expected to exercise reasonable care when handling MedDefense information and technology resources.

---

# 4. Prohibited Activities

The following activities are prohibited:

- Sharing usernames or passwords.
- Using another employee's account.
- Installing unauthorized software.
- Using unauthorized cloud storage services.
- Connecting personal network equipment.
- Bypassing security controls.
- Attempting to disable antivirus, EDR or logging software.
- Downloading pirated or illegal software.
- Running hacking tools without written authorization.
- Connecting unauthorized wireless access points.
- Circumventing MFA requirements.
- Connecting unauthorized IoT devices.
- Accessing patient information without a legitimate business need.
- Copying patient records for personal use.
- Attempting to exploit system vulnerabilities.

Violations may result in disciplinary action up to termination of employment.

---

# 5. Personal Devices and Removable Media

Because previous assessments identified Shadow IT and unrestricted USB usage as significant risks, the following rules apply.

## USB Devices

Personal USB storage devices are prohibited.

Only company-approved encrypted USB devices may be used and only when authorized by IT.

Unknown USB devices must never be connected to any MedDefense computer.

## Personal Mobile Phones

Personal phones may only connect to the approved guest wireless network.

Patient information must never be stored on personal devices.

Photography of patients or confidential information is prohibited unless explicitly authorized for medical purposes.

## Personal Laptops

Personal laptops may not connect to the internal MedDefense network.

Remote access must only occur through approved VPN solutions using MFA.

---

# 6. Password and Authentication Requirements

All users must comply with the following authentication requirements.

- Minimum password length: 14 characters.
- Passwords must be unique.
- Password reuse is prohibited.
- Default passwords must be changed immediately.
- Administrative accounts must use separate credentials.
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is mandatory for:
  - VPN access
  - Administrative accounts
  - Cloud services
  - Remote access
- Passwords must never be shared.
- Passwords must never be written on paper or stored in unsecured files.

---

# 7. Data Handling Requirements

MedDefense information shall be handled according to the organization's Data Classification Policy.

## Public

May be freely shared.

Examples:

- Website
- Marketing material

---

## Internal

For internal business use only.

Examples:

- Internal procedures
- Department documentation

Must not be shared outside MedDefense without authorization.

---

## Confidential

Includes:

- Employee records
- Financial information
- Internal reports

Requirements:

- Access only by authorized personnel.
- Encryption during transmission.
- Secure storage.

---

## Restricted

Includes:

- Patient medical records (PHI)
- Credentials
- Authentication secrets
- Encryption keys

Requirements:

- Strict least-privilege access.
- MFA required.
- Encryption at rest.
- Encryption in transit.
- Access logging enabled.
- Never copied to personal devices.
- Never emailed without approved encryption.

---

# 8. Email and Internet Usage

Employees shall:

- Verify suspicious emails before opening attachments.
- Report phishing emails immediately.
- Avoid clicking unknown links.
- Use only approved browsers.

Employees shall not:

- Download unauthorized software.
- Visit malicious or illegal websites.
- Forward confidential information to personal email accounts.

---

# 9. Incident Reporting

Any employee who suspects:

- Malware infection
- Phishing
- Lost laptop
- Lost badge
- Stolen credentials
- Unauthorized access
- Medical device malfunction caused by cyber activity

must immediately notify:

- IT Service Desk
- Security Analyst
- Deputy CISO

Prompt reporting is encouraged. Employees will not be disciplined for reporting incidents in good faith.

---

# 10. Monitoring and Privacy

To protect patient safety and organizational assets, MedDefense reserves the right to monitor:

- User logins
- VPN activity
- Internet usage
- Email metadata
- System logs
- Authentication events
- Network traffic
- Endpoint security alerts
- Administrative actions

Monitoring is performed solely for operational, security, regulatory and compliance purposes.

Users should have no expectation of personal privacy when using MedDefense systems.

Monitoring activities comply with applicable healthcare regulations and GDPR requirements.

---

# 11. Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in:

- Verbal warning
- Written warning
- Temporary suspension of system access
- Mandatory security awareness training
- Disciplinary action
- Employment termination
- Civil or criminal prosecution where applicable

Serious violations involving patient information, intentional misuse or unauthorized disclosure will be escalated immediately to executive management and legal counsel.

---

# 12. Exceptions

Any exception to this policy must:

- Be documented.
- Be approved by the Deputy CISO.
- Be approved by the IT Director.
- Include documented business justification.
- Include a risk assessment.
- Include an expiration date.
