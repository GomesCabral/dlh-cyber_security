# Part 3 - Alternative Allocation

## Alternative Allocation

The following **alternative allocation** uses a different combination of controls that achieves similar risk reduction at **lower cost**.

| Control | Cost |
|---|---:|

| Enterprise SIEM (Wazuh) | $25,000 |
| Offsite Immutable Backups | $18,000 |
| Network Segmentation | $40,000 |
| Medical Device Network Isolation | $28,000 |

**Alternative total spend:** $119,000

**Alternative budget remaining:** $1,000

---

## Compare the Alternative Allocation

The purpose of this section is to **compare** the alternative allocation with the primary recommendation.

### Primary Recommendation

- Total spend: $106,000
- Budget remaining: $14,000
- Higher overall ALE reduction
- Greater flexibility for emergency remediation
- Lower operational risk

### Alternative Allocation

- Total spend: $119,000
- Budget remaining: $1,000
- Better protection for medical devices
- Similar overall risk reduction
- Less flexibility for future spending

### Comparison

The primary recommendation achieves the highest overall reduction in Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE) while leaving contingency funds available.

The alternative allocation provides similar protection but at a **higher cost** and leaves almost no remaining budget.

Although both allocations reduce cyber risk, the primary recommendation is preferred because it delivers similar risk reduction at **lower cost** and provides greater financial flexibility for unexpected security incidents.
