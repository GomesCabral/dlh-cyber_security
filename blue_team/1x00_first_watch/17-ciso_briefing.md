# MedDefense Health Systems – Board Briefing

## Opening Statement

MedDefense is currently at high risk of a cyberattack that could interrupt patient care, expose confidential patient information and disrupt normal hospital operations. While some security measures are in place, they are not sufficient to protect the organization's most important systems.

## Critical Finding

Today, if an attacker gains control of a single computer, they could potentially move across much of the hospital's technology environment because there are very few internal barriers. This means one successful attack could affect patient records, medical equipment and critical hospital services at the same time.

## Three Priority Actions

**1. Separate critical hospital systems from everyday computers.** Estimated cost: **$35,000**. Timeline: **3–6 months**. This limits how far an attack can spread if one computer is compromised.

**2. Introduce continuous security monitoring.** Estimated cost: **$30,000**. Timeline: **1–3 months**. This allows suspicious activity to be detected early instead of only after patient care has already been affected.

**3. Protect older computers and medical equipment that can no longer receive security updates.** Estimated cost: **$25,000**. Timeline: **Within 1 month**. Additional safeguards will reduce the risk of these systems being used to attack the rest of the hospital.

## Business Case

The proposed **$120,000** investment is significantly lower than the cost of recovering from a major healthcare cyberattack, which can cost several million dollars, interrupt patient care for days and result in regulatory penalties and reputational damage.

## Closing

Delaying these improvements increases the likelihood of a cyberattack that could disrupt hospital services, compromise patient information and place patient safety at risk.
