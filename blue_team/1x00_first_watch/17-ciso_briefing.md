# MedDefense Health Systems – Board Briefing

## Opening Statement

MedDefense's current security posture presents significant business and patient safety risks. Basic security controls exist, but critical weaknesses leave the organization vulnerable to ransomware, data breaches and operational disruption.

## Critical Finding

The most serious issue is the lack of internal network segmentation. Once an attacker compromises a single computer, they can potentially move throughout the hospital network, reaching patient records, medical devices and critical systems. A successful attack could interrupt clinical services, expose protected patient information and significantly impact patient care.

## Three Priority Actions

**1. Implement internal network segmentation** – Estimated cost: **$35,000**. Timeline: **3–6 months**. Separating servers, workstations and medical devices will greatly reduce the spread of future attacks.

**2. Deploy centralized security monitoring** – Estimated cost: **$30,000**. Timeline: **1–3 months**. Continuous monitoring will allow suspicious activity to be detected early instead of after services have already been disrupted.

**3. Secure legacy systems and medical devices** – Estimated cost: **$25,000**. Timeline: **Within 1 month**. Isolating unsupported systems, replacing default passwords and strengthening access controls will reduce the highest patient safety risks.

## Business Case

The proposed **$120,000** security investment is significantly lower than the cost of a major healthcare cyberattack. Recent hospital ransomware incidents have resulted in millions of dollars in recovery costs, regulatory investigations, lost revenue and prolonged disruption to patient care.

## Closing

Failure to act leaves MedDefense exposed to preventable cyber incidents that could compromise patient safety, interrupt clinical operations and create substantial financial and reputational damage.
