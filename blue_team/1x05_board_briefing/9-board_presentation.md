# 9. Board Presentation

## Board Security Brief

### Current Threat Status

MedDefense is currently exposed to the **Crimson Tide** ransomware campaign, which is actively targeting healthcare organisations through vulnerable FortiGate SSL-VPN appliances. Based on the current environment, MedDefense falls within the campaign's threat profile and should treat the situation as an active operational incident rather than a theoretical cyber risk.

### Security Posture Verdict

MedDefense has established a solid cybersecurity foundation, including governance processes, security monitoring, backup capabilities and an overall risk management framework. However, several critical weaknesses—including an unpatched FortiGate appliance, limited network segmentation, legacy cryptographic configurations and incomplete encryption of sensitive data—must be addressed immediately to reduce the organisation's exposure.

### Emergency Response Summary

An emergency 72-hour response plan has been prepared to reduce the immediate risk posed by Crimson Tide. The first priority is upgrading the vulnerable FortiGate firewall, restricting SSL-VPN access where necessary and increasing monitoring for indicators of compromise. During the following 72 hours, privileged credentials will be rotated, backup integrity will be verified, forensic threat hunting will be completed and executive leadership will receive regular status updates. These actions are designed to minimise the likelihood of ransomware deployment while maintaining continuity of patient care.

### Investment Summary

Previous cybersecurity investments established essential security capabilities including governance, monitoring and backup processes, providing the foundation for the current response. The highest-priority immediate investment is renewing the FortiGate support contract for **$2,400**, enabling installation of the vendor security update that mitigates CVE-2023-27997. Compared with the updated **Annualised Loss Expectancy (ALE) of $400,000**, this investment provides an exceptional return and significantly reduces organisational risk. Additional investment over the next year should focus on network segmentation, encryption at rest, immutable backups and identity security.

### Recommendation

My professional recommendation is to approve the emergency response plan and associated funding immediately. Rapid execution of the 72-hour plan, followed by the accelerated 30-day remediation roadmap, will significantly reduce MedDefense's exposure while strengthening long-term resilience against ransomware and future cyber threats.
