# MedDefense — Patient Information Security Incident

**Board briefing | 21 April 2026, 07:00 UTC (requested delivery)**  
**Presented by:** Dr. Patricia Morales  
**Prepared:** Sunday, 20 April 2026, 23:00 UTC  
**Status:** Preliminary — investigation ongoing  
**Confidential — Board and authorized leadership only**

## What happened

On Sunday evening, MedDefense received reports of abnormal behavior on a clinical workstation and the patient scheduling server at its main site. Security monitoring also identified suspicious software installations on three additional clinical workstations. The incident response team opened a critical-priority investigation. These reports and alerts are documented in the initial call record; the full cause and extent have not yet been independently established.

## Patient information potentially affected

The incident scenario identifies access to **1,847 patient files**, including laboratory results and clinical notes, on the clinical file server. **The number of individual patients affected has not been verified.** The current forensic report is preliminary and does not establish whether information was transferred outside MedDefense.

## Patient services

The scheduling server was reported to be operating abnormally. **The extent of any disruption to patient appointments or clinical care has not yet been verified.** There is no verified finding in the available record that patient treatment was interrupted.

## Legal and notification position

**The available evidence does not support concluding that the patient information was at low risk of compromise; legal review is pending, and notification obligations must be assessed before any final determination is announced.**

## Response and outstanding work

The response team opened a critical-priority incident and documented the initial reports. A memory copy from the first affected workstation was reported as collected before the response call; its integrity verification remains outstanding. A coordinated plan to limit unauthorized access and protect patient services has been prepared, but successful isolation, access revocation and completion of the investigation have **not** been independently verified in the available record.

**Priorities now:** verify that unauthorized access has stopped; establish the number of affected patients; confirm whether any information left the organization; assess any effect on patient services; and obtain the formal legal determination.
