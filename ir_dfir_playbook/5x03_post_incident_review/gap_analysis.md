# Gap Analysis: IR-2026-0414-01

**Organization:** MedDefense  
**Source documents:** `control_effectiveness.yaml` (Task 1) and `forensic_findings_summary.md` (5x02 hand-off)  
**Scope:** Credential phishing, foreign-IP identity activity, PowerShell/MSBuild execution, C2, credential access, lateral movement and protected health information (PHI) access.

## Assessment method

- **P1 critical:** A gap associated with a confirmed attack path, credential compromise, persistent execution, C2, or access to PHI that needs urgent containment or a high-impact preventive/detective improvement.
- **P2 high:** A material detection, hardening or investigation gap needing scheduled remediation, where the failure of a specific deployed control is not fully evidenced.
- **P3 medium:** A useful follow-up improvement with lower immediate impact.
- **Evidence qualification:** A confirmed malicious action does **not** by itself prove a specific security product was installed and failed. Where the source does not contain alert, policy, VPN, or DLP logs, the gap is framed as **missing demonstrated coverage / validation required**, not as a proven failure. Recommendations below are proposed controls, not claims that those products already exist.

## Priority summary

- **GAP-001 (P1):** The known phishing domain was used to harvest credentials; blocking that exact destination reduces exposure at the earliest observed attack stage.
- **GAP-002 (P1):** Suppressed Entra risk signals and compromised credentials demand urgent conditional-access and phishing-resistant MFA review.
- **GAP-004 (P1):** The confirmed PowerShell-to-MSBuild execution chain warrants a tested, high-signal endpoint detection.
- **GAP-005 (P1):** Execution of an MSBuild loader and payload from a user-writable Temp path justifies application-control enforcement after compatibility testing.
- **GAP-006 (P1):** Confirmed outbound C2 connections justify immediate IOC blocking and tighter clinical-workstation egress policy.
- **GAP-007 (P1):** Process-aware detection of MSBuild outbound HTTPS traffic can reveal C2 even when an IP-specific block becomes stale.
- **GAP-010 (P1):** Confirmed workstation-to-workstation lateral movement warrants segmentation and remote-administration restrictions.
- **GAP-011 (P1):** Fourteen confirmed PHI PDF reads require rapid anomalous-access detection and a review of share permissions.

## Evidence limits and implementation hand-off

The findings summary does not establish that VPN access occurred, that a particular security vendor product was deployed, that an LSASS dump succeeded, or that PHI was exfiltrated. GAP-003, GAP-008, GAP-009 and GAP-012 therefore explicitly require verification. The gap register recommends specific technical changes, but the assigned owner, deadline, success criterion and validation evidence should be recorded in the subsequent remediation tracker before these actions are considered complete.
